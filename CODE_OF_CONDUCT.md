# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe environment.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
